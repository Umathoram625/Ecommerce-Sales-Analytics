Analytics Workflow

01 · Data Preparation

Cleaned and structured the source data into analytical datasets suitable for downstream analysis.

02 · SQL Analysis

Built business queries to investigate sales, products, customers and geographic performance.

03 · RFM Segmentation

Segmented customers using Recency, Frequency and Monetary behavior to identify retention opportunities.

04 · Power BI

Designed a star-schema analytical model and DAX-powered reporting framework for decision making.

Key Business Insights

The following recommendations are based on the validated analysis completed in the project.

Discount Optimization Analysis identified approximately $156.1K lost from discounts above 20%, supporting a hard-cap discount strategy.
Furniture Rationalization Tables and Bookcases showed approximately $21.2K in losses, supporting SKU and product-bundle optimization.
Customer Retention Approximately $445.8K of revenue was identified as being at risk from customers likely to lapse.
Regional Performance Approximately $78.4K in losses were identified across TX, OH, PA, IL and NC, highlighting regional optimization opportunities.
$160K–$215K

Estimated total bottom-line uplift from the combined discount, product, customer-retention and regional recommendations.
