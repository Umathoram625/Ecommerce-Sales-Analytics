Key Business Insights

Discount Optimization

Approximately $156.1K in losses were associated with discounts above 20%. The analysis supports introducing tighter discount controls.

Customer Retention

Approximately $445.8K of revenue was identified as being at risk, highlighting the importance of targeted retention campaigns.

Furniture Rationalization

Tables and Bookcases generated approximately $21.2K in losses, indicating an opportunity for product and SKU-level optimization.

Regional Performance

Approximately $78.4K in losses were identified across TX, OH, PA, IL and NC, creating an opportunity for regional performance improvement.

$160K – $215K

Estimated impact from the combined discount, customer retention, product and regional recommendations.
