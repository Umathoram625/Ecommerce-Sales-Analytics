## 2. Core Business Findings & Empirical Metrics

### A. Profitability Concentration by Category
- **Technology** is the primary profit engine, generating **$836,154.03** in sales and **$145,454.95** in net profit (**17.39% margin**). Key growth sub-categories include Copiers ($55.6K profit, 36.6% margin) and Phones ($44.5K profit, 13.5% margin).
- **Office Supplies** exhibits high volume and stable returns, delivering **$719,047.03** in sales and **$122,490.80** in profit (**17.03% margin**), anchored by Paper ($34.1K profit, 43.4% margin) and Binders ($30.2K profit, 14.9% margin).
- **Furniture** represents a major operational drag: despite driving **$741,999.80** in revenue (32.3% of total company sales), it yielded only **$18,451.27** in net profit (**2.49% margin**). Sub-category **Tables** lost **-$17,725.48** and **Bookcases** lost **-$3,472.56**.

### B. The Discount Margin Destruction Threshold
- Orders with **0% discount** deliver a robust **30.0% profit margin**.
- Orders with **1%–20% discount** deliver a sound **21.0% margin**.
- Orders discounted **21%–50%** plunge into deficits at **-18.5% margin**.
- Orders discounted **>50%** collapse to **-85.2% margin**, destroying **-$156,128.33** in cumulative profits across 1,871 transactions.

### C. Customer Lifetime Value & RFM Segmentation
- **793 unique customers** generated an average lifetime revenue of **$2,896.85**.
- **Champions & Loyal Customers** (33.4% of customer base) generate **49.3% of total revenue** ($1.13M).
- **At Risk Cohort**: 101 high-value accounts representing **$445,804.88 in revenue (19.4%)** with an attractive **16.22% profit margin** have become dormant (>180 days since last purchase).

### D. Regional & State Performance Disparities
- **West Region** leads the business in profitability with **$108,418.45** in profit (14.9% margin), followed by the **East Region** ($91,522.78, 13.5% margin).
- **Deficit States**: 10 states operate at a net loss due to extreme discounting (>30% avg discount): Texas (-$25,729 loss), Ohio (-$16,971 loss), Pennsylvania (-$15,560 loss), and Illinois (-$12,607 loss).
